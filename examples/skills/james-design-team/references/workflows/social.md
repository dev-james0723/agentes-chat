# Social production

## Prepare the campaign

Identify the brand, audience, objective, actual channels, languages, required formats, timeline and task authorization. Reuse the current brief and approved brand samples. Keep source facts separate from creative claims; record a source link or document location for factual statements, quotations, prices and dates.

Build one coherent campaign angle, then adapt the hook, body, call to action and composition for each channel. Do not assume that identical copy or a cropped square works everywhere. Verify current channel specifications when the task depends on them.

## Make the requested outputs

- Assign an execution owner for the visual package and a copy collaborator when needed. Keep a clear owner for each deliverable.
- Draft captions in the requested register, including Hong Kong written Traditional Chinese or Cantonese where appropriate. Preserve names, facts and offers across languages.
- Create real imagery, layouts, carousels, stories or rendered video through available authorized tools. Prompts, concepts and caption drafts remain intermediate work.
- Retain editable source and export each requested variant. Check text at the intended viewing size, crop boundaries, UI overlays, contrast, alt text and captions where applicable.
- Keep asset filenames and the content version aligned with the campaign record. A change to the image or copy creates a new reviewable version.

The independent reviewer checks the actual visuals and copy together against the same source facts. A caption-only review does not complete a visual campaign. Follow [typography](typography.md), [review](review.md) and [delivery](delivery.md).

## Publication and learning

Prepare authorized publication with the correct account, channel, assets and content version. Production authorization alone does not create permission to post publicly; honor any explicit publication authorization already present in the task. Record the platform result and read it back before claiming published or retrying an ambiguous operation.

After publication, distinguish actual metrics from hypotheses. Record observation dates, denominator and channel context; one post's performance is not causal evidence that a creative choice worked.
